OUR SERVICES

Dental Treatments

Comprehensive care for healthy, confident smiles.

01

Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

Professional teeth cleaning helps remove plaque, tartar and buildup from teeth and below the gum line. Regular cleaning supports healthy teeth and gums.

02

Laser Dentistry

At Sakthi Dental Clinic, advanced laser technology is used for precise, minimally invasive dental treatments. From soft tissue surgeries to gum care, laser procedures can offer faster healing, reduced discomfort and enhanced accuracy.

03

Root Canal Therapy

When tooth infections reach deep into the pulp, root canal treatment becomes essential. The infected tissue is removed, the area is cleaned and the tooth is sealed to prevent future issues.

04

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

If impacted or problematic, wisdom teeth can cause discomfort and oral health risks. Modern techniques and anesthesia options help provide a smooth experience, followed by post-operative care.

05

Fixed Partial Denture (Bridge)

Fixed partial dentures provide a secure solution for replacing missing teeth by anchoring prosthetic teeth to adjacent natural teeth or implants.

06

Teeth Whitening (Bleaching)

Professional teeth whitening treatments help address discoloration and brighten your smile using safe bleaching agents.

07

Veneers

Dental veneers are thin, custom-made shells designed to cover imperfections such as chips, gaps or discoloration and improve the appearance of teeth.

08

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle and comprehensive dental care for children. Our friendly approach creates a welcoming environment where young patients feel safe and comfortable.

09

Flap Surgery

For advanced gum disease, flap surgery may be necessary. The procedure helps remove deep-seated plaque and bacteria and supports improved gum health.

10

Orthodontic Braces

Correct misaligned teeth with customized orthodontic treatments. Options can include metal, ceramic or lingual braces to improve alignment and bite function.

11

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners provide a discreet alternative to traditional braces. These transparent removable trays gradually move teeth into alignment.

12

Dental Implants

Dental implants restore the appearance and functionality of missing teeth, providing a stable and natural-looking solution.
